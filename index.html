Official Rulebook

Rules, Regulations & Code of Conduct

All participants must strictly review and adhere to the guidelines established by the GDG On Campus PSNACET steering committee.

Eligibility & Registration Process

  • Open to All College Students: Undergraduate and postgraduate engineering and tech students from any recognized university/institution across India are eligible.
  • Official Confirmation Requirement: Registration has to be submitted by the Team Leader. The fee of ₹150 per member must only be paid after receiving the official shortlist confirmation email from gdscpsna@psnacet.edu.in.
  • Valid College ID: All physical attendees must produce their original college identity cards during on-campus physical verification at the registration desk.
  • Inter-College Teams: Inter-college team formations are permitted, provided all participants have active student statuses.

Team Composition & Roles

  • Team Size: Teams must strictly consist of 4 members as specified in the official ARENACORE '26 guidelines. Solo participation is not permitted for ARENACORE '26 to encourage collaborative teamwork.
  • Team Leader: Every team must designate one Team Leader who will act as the single point of contact for submissions, announcements, and communications.
  • Unique Team Identity: Each team must pick a unique team name that does not infringe on registered trademarks or contain offensive language.
  • No Dual Registrations: A participant cannot be registered in more than one team simultaneously.

Project Prototype & Intellectual Property

  • Pre-Existing Code Policy: Projects can be conceptualized beforehand and brought with partial prototypes / architecture blueprints; however, significant development, feature integration, and final refinement must be completed during the hackathon.
  • Open Source & Libraries: Teams may utilize open-source libraries, APIs, and frameworks (such as Google Cloud APIs, TensorFlow, Gemini, Firebase, React, Flutter) with proper citations.
  • 100% IP Ownership: Participants retain 100% intellectual property ownership of their projects and code. Organizers are only granted rights to showcase projects for promotional, educational, and reporting purposes.
  • Submission Artifacts: Teams must submit a working GitHub repository URL, a 3-minute demo video/slides, and provide a live functional demonstration to judges during jury rounds.

Code of Conduct & Campus Etiquette

  • Inclusivity & Mutual Respect: GDG is committed to providing a safe, harassment-free environment for everyone regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, or religion.
  • Formal Dress Code: In accordance with PSNACET campus guidelines, all participants must follow a neat, formal / semi-formal dress code throughout the event.
  • Campus Regulations: Possession of prohibited substances or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification and reporting to respective college authorities.
  • Facility Care: Participants are expected to treat college lab computers, network equipment, and furniture with utmost care.
