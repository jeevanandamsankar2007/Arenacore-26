Official Rulebook & Protocols

ArenaCore ’26 Hackathon Guidelines

Please review the 18 official guidelines below. Adherence to all rules, eligibility criteria, and professional conduct is mandatory for all participating teams.

RULE 01 Eligibility

1. Eligibility

Quick Take: Open to UG/PG • 4 Members/Squad • Free to Apply
  • ArenaCore ’26 is open to students currently pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate programs in recognized educational institutions.
  • Each team must consist of up to 4 members.
  • All participants must register individually as part of their respective team.
  • A participant can be part of only one team.
Key Takeaway: Open to all enrolled UG/PG students. Up to 4 members per team; dual registrations across teams are strictly forbidden.
RULE 02 Registration

2. Registration

Quick Take: 1 Theme per squad • Locked at Registration Deadline
  • Teams must complete the official registration form with accurate participant and institutional details.
  • Registration will be considered complete only after all required information has been submitted.
  • Teams must select one theme during registration.
  • Theme selection cannot be changed after the registration deadline unless permitted by the organizing committee. (Selected teams will receive the payment form after the selection process is completed.)
Theme Lock: Theme selection is locked at the deadline. Payment forms will follow after team selection is finalized.
RULE 03 Fee & Selection

3. Registration Fee

Quick Take: ₹500/member • Pay ONLY if Shortlisted (Cap of 10 teams/theme)
  • The registration fee is ₹500 per participant.
  • Therefore, the total registration fee is ₹2,000 per team (for a total of 4 members).
  • Payment instructions will be communicated by the organizing team after registration/selection.
  • Registration fees, once paid, are non-refundable.
Team Selection & Payment Protocol:
  • Only 10 teams will be selected under each theme.
  • Selected teams will receive the payment form and payment instructions.
  • Registration does not guarantee selection. The registration fee will be collected only from selected teams.
  • Selected teams must complete payment within the specified deadline to confirm participation.
Strict Cap: Only 10 teams per theme! Fee (₹500/member) is collected exclusively from shortlisted teams.
RULE 04 Format

4. Event Format

Quick Take: 24 Continuous Hours • Non-Stop Sprint at PSNACET IT Campus
  • ArenaCore ’26 is a 24-hour continuous hackathon.
  • The hackathon will begin and end at the officially announced timings.
  • Teams are expected to develop their solution during the designated hackathon period.
  • Problem statements and detailed requirements will be provided according to the selected theme.
Continuous Sprint: 24 continuous hours across Oct 27 & 28, 2026. Teams work directly on theme-assigned challenges.
RULE 05 Themes

5. Problem Statement & Theme

Quick Take: Freedom of Innovation • Practical Solutions & UN SDG Alignment
  • Each team must work on the problem statement assigned under its selected theme.
  • Teams are encouraged to identify meaningful problems, understand the requirements, and develop practical technology-driven solutions.
  • Solutions should demonstrate originality, creativity, and meaningful implementation.
  • Teams must comply with any additional requirements specified in their problem statement.
Focus on Practicality: Originality, creative problem solving, and meaningful real-world utility are strongly evaluated.
RULE 06 Development

6. Development Rules

Quick Take: Build during the hack • AI allowed as assistant, no pre-built projects
  • The core development work must be carried out during the official hackathon period.
  • Teams may use publicly available documentation, libraries, frameworks, APIs, and open-source tools where their licenses permit such use.
  • AI-assisted development tools may be used as supporting tools; however, teams must understand, implement, and demonstrate their own solution.
  • Direct submission of copied projects, existing complete solutions, or plagiarized work is strictly prohibited.
  • Any violation may result in disqualification.
AI Tool Policy: AI tools may only support your workflow. You must deeply understand and defend every line of code submitted.
RULE 07 Collaboration

7. Team Collaboration

Quick Take: Active contribution from all 4 members • Team presentation required
  • All four registered members must actively contribute to the project.
  • Teams should be prepared to explain each member's contribution during evaluation.
  • Team members may divide responsibilities such as development, UI/UX, research, testing, documentation, and presentation.
Active Contribution: All 4 members must have defined responsibilities and be ready to articulate them to the jury.
RULE 08 Equipment

8. Equipment & Connectivity

Quick Take: Bring your laptops & hardware • 1 Gbps Wi-Fi & labs provided
  • Participants must bring their own laptops, chargers, and required accessories.
  • Teams are responsible for their own devices and personal belongings.
  • Internet connectivity will be available at the venue.
  • Participants may use personal mobile data as an alternative where required.
  • Participants should ensure that all necessary development tools, software, SDKs, and dependencies are installed before the event.
Pre-Install Reminder: Download compilers, npm packages, Docker images, and datasets prior to campus arrival.
RULE 09 Conduct

9. Venue Conduct

Quick Take: Professional conduct • Zero tolerance for disruptions or vandalism
  • Participants must follow the instructions of the organizing committee, faculty coordinators, mentors, and volunteers.
  • Professional and respectful behavior is expected throughout the event.
  • Any form of harassment, cheating, vandalism, disruptive behavior, or intentional interference with another team's work may result in disciplinary action or disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain cleanliness and take proper care of the venue and facilities.
Zero Tolerance: Respectful, professional conduct is non-negotiable. Disruptions or vandalism will result in instant disqualification.
RULE 10 Overnight

10. Breaks & Overnight Participation

Quick Take: Plan shifts & breaks • At least 1 member stationed at table
  • Teams may take breaks during the 24-hour event.
  • Participants are responsible for managing their team's working schedule effectively.
  • At least one member of every team must remain available at the designated workstation during the active hackathon period, unless otherwise permitted by the organizers.
Shift Planning: Rest areas and refreshments will be provided. Keep at least one team member stationed at your table at all active hours.
RULE 11 Mentorship

11. Mentorship

Quick Take: 24/7 GDE & Alumni Mentors available on deck
  • Mentors may be available during designated periods to provide technical or domain guidance.
  • Mentors may offer suggestions, but teams are responsible for their own technical decisions and implementation.
  • Mentorship does not guarantee any particular evaluation outcome.
Expert Guidance: Consult domain mentors during dedicated rounds to validate technical architecture and design choices.
RULE 12 Submission

12. Submission

Quick Take: Working MVP + GitHub repo + Slide deck before 24h timer
  • Before the official submission deadline, each team must submit the required project materials specified by the organizing committee.
  • The submission may include:
    • Working project / prototype
    • Source code or repository (e.g. GitHub)
    • Project description & architecture notes
    • Presentation / demo materials
    • Any additional documentation requested by the organizers
  • Late submissions may not be considered for evaluation unless an extension is officially announced.
Hard Deadline: Code freeze is exact at 24 hours. Commit early to avoid last-minute network congestion.
RULE 13 Evaluation Rubric & Factors

13. Evaluation

Quick Take: 100 Pts: Tech 30%, Innovation 25%, Impact 25%, Pitch 20%

Projects will be evaluated by the jury based on factors including:

Innovation & Creativity

Originality of the idea and creativity of the proposed solution.

Technical Implementation

Quality of development, architecture, technology usage, and implementation.

Functionality

Working capability of the prototype and how effectively it addresses the problem.

Feasibility & Impact

Practicality, scalability, potential usefulness, and real-world impact.

User Experience

Quality of interface, usability, accessibility, and overall experience where applicable.

Presentation & Demo

Clarity of explanation, live demonstration, and the team's ability to answer questions from the jury.

Note: The evaluation structure and weightage may be communicated separately by the organizing committee.

RULE 14 Final Pitch

14. Final Presentation

Quick Take: Live functional demo required • All 4 squad members speak
  • Every shortlisted team will be given a designated presentation/evaluation slot.
  • Teams must demonstrate their working solution to the jury.
  • The presentation should clearly explain the problem, proposed solution, technology used, implementation, and impact.
  • All team members should be prepared to participate in the presentation and/or technical discussion.
Live Demo Mandatory: Pre-recorded videos can support, but a live functional demonstration is required. All 4 members must speak.
RULE 15 Intellectual Property

15. Intellectual Property & Originality

Quick Take: You retain 100% IP of your code • License third-party libraries
  • Teams are responsible for ensuring that their submitted work does not infringe the intellectual property rights of others.
  • Third-party libraries, APIs, datasets, and open-source components must be used according to their respective licenses.
  • Any intellectual property arising from a team's project will remain subject to applicable institutional and participant agreements.
License Compliance: Comply with MIT, Apache 2.0, or other relevant licenses. Do not infringe copyrighted material or trademarks.
RULE 16 Disqualification

16. Disqualification

Quick Take: Strict disqualification for plagiarism or pre-built submissions

A team may be disqualified for:

  • Plagiarism or substantial copying of another project.
  • Submission of a pre-built solution as the primary hackathon work.
  • Providing false registration information.
  • Cheating or unfair practices.
  • Serious misconduct or violation of event rules.
  • Tampering with another team's work or event infrastructure.
  • Failure to follow instructions from the organizing committee.
Strict Integrity: Disqualification decisions are permanent and result in forfeiture of certificates and event privileges.
RULE 17 Prizes

17. Prizes & Recognition

Quick Take: ₹1,20,000 cash pool + verified GDG & ACM certificates
  • Winning teams will receive cash prizes, certificates, and recognition.
  • Certificates will be provided to eligible participants as specified by the organizing committee.
  • Prize distribution will be subject to verification of team eligibility and compliance with the hackathon rules.
  • The organizing committee reserves the right to make necessary decisions regarding prizes in exceptional circumstances.
₹1,20,000 Prize Pool: Verified awards and official GDG x ACM certificates are awarded following formal verification.
RULE 18 Authority

18. Organizing Committee

Quick Take: Organizing committee decisions are final & binding

The organizing committee reserves the right to:

  • Modify the event schedule when necessary.
  • Clarify or update rules during the event.
  • Take appropriate action in situations not explicitly covered in these guidelines.
  • Make the final decision regarding rule violations, eligibility, submissions, and event-related disputes.
Final Authority: Decisions by the organizing committee on scheduling, operational disputes, and rule clarifications are final.
