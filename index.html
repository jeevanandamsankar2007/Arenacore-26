Official Rulebook & Protocols

ArenaCore ’26 Hackathon Guidelines

Please review the 10 official guidelines below. Adherence to all rules, eligibility criteria, and professional conduct is mandatory for all participating teams.

RULE 01 Eligibility

1. Eligibility

Quick Take: Strictly Engineering Undergraduates • 2–4 Members/Squad • Free to Apply
  • ArenaCore ’26 is strictly open to students currently pursuing engineering undergraduate programs (B.E. / B.Tech) in recognized educational institutions.
  • Each team must consist of 2 to 4 members.
  • All participants must register individually as part of their respective team.
  • A participant can be part of only one team.
Key Takeaway: Strictly open to students pursuing engineering undergraduate degrees (B.E. / B.Tech). 2 to 4 members per team; dual registrations across teams are strictly forbidden.
RULE 02 Registration & Fees

2. Registration & Registration Fee

Quick Take: 1 Theme per squad • ₹500/member • Free Food, Stay & Kits included
  • Teams must complete the official registration form with accurate participant and institutional details.
  • Teams must select one theme during registration. Theme selection cannot be changed after the registration deadline unless permitted by the organizing committee.
  • The registration fee is ₹500 per participant (for teams of 2 to 4 members; e.g., ₹1,000 for 2 members, ₹2,000 for 4 members).
  • What is Covered: Free food and refreshments, free accommodation, and official event kits are provided for all registered participants.
  • Pay ONLY if Selected: Registration does not guarantee selection. The registration fee will be collected only from selected teams (exactly 10 teams per theme). Selected teams will receive the payment form and instructions after the selection process is completed.
  • Selected teams must complete payment within the specified deadline to confirm participation. Registration fees, once paid, are non-refundable.
Participant Perks Included with Registration:
  • Free Food & Refreshments: Nutritious meals, snacks, and continuous beverages throughout the 24-hour sprint.
  • Free Accommodation: Safe campus stay arrangements provided for all participants.
  • Hacker Kits & Swag: Official event kits, ID passes, certificates, and badges will be provided.
Strict Cap: Only 10 teams per theme! Fee (₹500/member) is collected exclusively from shortlisted teams, with food, stay, and kits fully included.
RULE 03 Development & Equipment

3. Development Rules & Equipment

Quick Take: Build on-site • Bring own laptops & accessories • AI allowed as assistant
  • The core development work must be carried out during the official hackathon period.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops, chargers, and required accessories; teams are solely responsible for their personal devices.
  • Campus Wi-Fi will be available at the venue; participants may also use mobile data as backup where needed.
  • Teams may use publicly available libraries, frameworks, APIs, and open-source tools compliant with their licenses.
  • Participants should ensure development environments, SDKs, and dependencies are pre-installed before arrival.
  • AI-assisted tools may be used as supporting tools, but teams must fully understand, implement, and defend their own solution.
  • Direct submission of copied projects, existing pre-built applications, or plagiarized work is strictly prohibited.
Development & Integrity: Build your prototype on-site. AI tools may only assist your workflow; pre-built submissions will result in immediate disqualification.
RULE 04 Evaluation Rubric & Factors

4. Evaluation

Quick Take: 100 Pts: Tech 30%, Innovation 25%, Impact 25%, Pitch 20%

Projects will be evaluated by the jury based on factors including:

Innovation & Creativity

Originality of the idea and creativity of the proposed solution.

Technical Implementation

Quality of development, architecture, technology usage, and implementation.

Functionality

Working capability of the prototype and how effectively it addresses the problem.

Feasibility & Impact

Practicality, scalability, potential usefulness, and real-world impact.

User Experience

Quality of interface, usability, accessibility, and overall experience where applicable.

Presentation & Demo

Clarity of explanation, live demonstration, and the team's ability to answer questions from the jury.

Note: The evaluation structure and weightage may be communicated separately by the organizing committee.

RULE 05 Final Pitch

5. Final Presentation

Quick Take: Live functional demo required • All 4 squad members speak
  • Every shortlisted team will be given a designated presentation/evaluation slot.
  • Teams must demonstrate their working solution to the jury.
  • The presentation should clearly explain the problem, proposed solution, technology used, implementation, and impact.
  • All team members should be prepared to participate in the presentation and/or technical discussion.
Live Demo Mandatory: Pre-recorded videos can support, but a live functional demonstration is required. All team members must speak.
RULE 06 Intellectual Property

6. Intellectual Property & Originality

Quick Take: You retain 100% IP of your code • License third-party libraries
  • Teams are responsible for ensuring that their submitted work does not infringe the intellectual property rights of others.
  • Third-party libraries, APIs, datasets, and open-source components must be used according to their respective licenses.
  • Any intellectual property arising from a team's project will remain subject to applicable institutional and participant agreements.
License Compliance: Comply with MIT, Apache 2.0, or other relevant licenses. Do not infringe copyrighted material or trademarks.
RULE 07 Disqualification

7. Disqualification

Quick Take: Strict disqualification for plagiarism or pre-built submissions

A team may be disqualified for:

  • Plagiarism or substantial copying of another project.
  • Submission of a pre-built solution as the primary hackathon work.
  • Providing false registration information.
  • Cheating or unfair practices.
  • Serious misconduct or violation of event rules.
  • Tampering with another team's work or event infrastructure.
  • Failure to follow instructions from the organizing committee.
Strict Integrity: Disqualification decisions are permanent and result in forfeiture of certificates and event privileges.
RULE 08 Prizes

8. Prizes & Recognition

Quick Take: ₹40,000 per theme (Winner: ₹25,000 • Runner: ₹15,000) • Total ₹1,20,000
  • Total cash prize pool is ₹1,20,000, distributed equally as ₹40,000 for each theme across all 3 official hackathon tracks.
  • For each theme: Winner receives ₹25,000 cash + trophy + merit certificates, and Runner-Up receives ₹15,000 cash + trophy + certificates.
  • Certificates of Participation will be provided to all eligible attendees who present their projects during the final pitch.
  • Prize distribution will be subject to verification of team eligibility and compliance with the hackathon rules.
  • The organizing committee reserves the right to make necessary decisions regarding prizes in exceptional circumstances.
₹40,000 Prize Pool / Theme (Winner: ₹25,000 | Runner-Up: ₹15,000): Verified cash awards allocated per theme and official GDG x ACM certificates are awarded following formal verification.
RULE 09 Authority

9. Organizing Committee

Quick Take: Organizing committee decisions are final & binding

The organizing committee reserves the right to:

  • Modify the event schedule when necessary.
  • Clarify or update rules during the event.
  • Take appropriate action in situations not explicitly covered in these guidelines.
  • Make the final decision regarding rule violations, eligibility, submissions, and event-related disputes.
Final Authority: Decisions by the organizing committee on scheduling, operational disputes, and rule clarifications are final.
